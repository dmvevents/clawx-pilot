MINISTRY OF EDUCATION
REPUBLIC OF TRINIDAD AND TOBAGO

CIRCULAR MEMORANDUM No. 47 of 2026

TO:        All Principals, Primary Schools
FROM:      Chief Education Officer
DATE:      3rd September 2026
SUBJECT:   Revised Procedures for the Primary School Daily Report Submission

1. This Circular advises all Primary School Principals across the seven
   education districts (Caroni; North Eastern; Port of Spain & Environs;
   South Eastern; St. George East; St. Patrick; Victoria) of revised
   procedures for the submission of the Primary School Daily Report.

2. With effect from Monday 15th September 2026, the Daily Report must be
   submitted through the Ministry's online form no later than 3:45 p.m. on
   each school day. Late submissions will no longer be accepted by the
   District Office by telephone.

3. Principals are reminded that attendance figures must reflect the actual
   roll call taken at the start of the school day and must separately record
   pupils present, pupils absent, and the teaching staff on duty.

4. District Education Officers will monitor compliance for the term and
   report exceptions to the Chief Education Officer.

5. Kindly ensure the contents of this Circular are brought to the attention
   of all members of staff.

By Order of the Chief Education Officer
Ministry of Education
