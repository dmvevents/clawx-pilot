# Connect your email and forms

For people using the Ministry of Education desktop app on Windows.

Use your own work or school Microsoft account. Sign in to Outlook before asking the assistant to read email or prepare replies. If your school uses Microsoft Forms, you also need access to the forms supplied by your school or Ministry.

## Sign in to Outlook in Chrome

1. Open the Ministry of Education app in your usual Windows account and ask **“Open Outlook.”**
2. In the Chrome window used by the app, sign in to Outlook with your work or school account. Enter your password only on Microsoft's sign-in page and complete any verification Microsoft requests.
3. Wait until your inbox appears. Open the account menu in Outlook and check that it shows the account you want the assistant to use.
4. Keep that Chrome window available, return to the app and ask **“Show my recent emails.”** Check that the result agrees with your inbox.

If you already signed in using another Chrome profile or another Windows account, the app's Chrome window may still need its own sign-in. Check the account in that window before continuing. Signing into Windows or a remote desktop does not sign you into Outlook.

Enter your Microsoft password only on the Microsoft sign-in page; never paste it into the assistant's chat.

## If your school has enabled Microsoft 365 sign-in in the app

Open **Settings → Microsoft 365 sign-in**, select **Sign in**, and complete the Microsoft sign-in page using your own account. Check the account shown in Settings when you return.

If Settings asks for administrator configuration, contact your school's IT support. They provide the organisation's setup; you do not need to create an application registration yourself.

This connection lets supported email actions use Microsoft 365 directly. It is separate from signing into Outlook in Chrome. Forms and other browser tasks may still ask you to sign in in Chrome.

## Set up your forms

1. Open **Settings → Principal setup**.
2. Add the **Daily Report response link** and **Student Suspensions response link** provided by your school or Ministry, then select **Save form links**.
3. Open the relevant form in the same Chrome profile used by the app. If the form asks you to sign in, use your authorised work or school account. Confirm that the questions appear; being signed into Outlook alone does not guarantee access to a particular form.
4. Ask the assistant to prepare the report you need. Review every proposed answer before confirming submission.

## Review before sending

Ask for a draft when preparing email. Check the recipients, subject, message and attachments before explicitly confirming a send. Review form answers before explicitly confirming submission. Reading an inbox, preparing a draft or viewing a form is not confirmation to send or submit it.

## If something does not work

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| A Microsoft sign-in page appears again | Sign in again in the Chrome window used by the app, complete any verification, then retry your request. |
| The wrong account or inbox appears | Save any unfinished work, switch to the intended account in that Chrome profile, and check the account menu before retrying. If the app uses Microsoft 365 sign-in, also check its account in Settings. |
| A form says you do not have access | Check that you are using the right account and response link. Ask the form owner or school IT for access. |
| Microsoft requests administrator approval | Contact school IT with the approval message. |
| Chrome fails to open, or the app still cannot reach an inbox you can see | Save your work and retry once using the app's instructions. If it still fails, contact support with the app version, time and error message. |

For support, share a screenshot of the error with account details and message contents hidden. Never include passwords, verification codes or private form links in a support screenshot.
